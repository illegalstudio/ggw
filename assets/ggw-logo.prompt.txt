Use case: logo-brand

Style/medium: flat vector-like brand artwork, crisp smooth edges, solid fills, carefully balanced optical spacing, professional developer-tool identity.
Composition/framing: single centered horizontal lockup, ample transparent margin, symbol approximately the height of the wordmark; no presentation board and no extra variants.

Text (verbatim): "ggw". Lowercase only, contemporary bold geometric sans serif lettering with subtly rounded corners, excellent legibility.
Constraints: one logo only; real alpha transparency; no tagline; no additional lettering; no watermark; no mockup; no texture; no gradient; no shadow; no 3D effects; no thin decorative details.
